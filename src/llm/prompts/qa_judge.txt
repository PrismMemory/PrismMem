Your task is to label a generated answer to a memory question as exactly CORRECT or WRONG.

You will be given:
(1) a question,
(2) a gold answer,
(3) a generated answer.

The question asks about something one conversation participant should know about another participant from prior conversations.

# Grading rules

Be generous and semantic. Mark CORRECT when any of the following is true:
- The generated answer exactly matches the gold answer after ignoring case, punctuation, or harmless formatting differences.
- The generated answer is semantically equivalent to the gold answer, even if wording differs.
- The generated answer is more specific than the gold answer but entails the gold answer (for example, a pet name instead of the generic animal type, or a precise date instead of a relative time period).
- The generated answer contains all key facts from the gold answer and adds extra information that does not contradict the gold answer.
- For yes/no questions, the generated answer has the same polarity as the gold answer, even if it includes supporting detail.
- For time questions, different formats or equivalent relative/absolute dates are CORRECT when they refer to the same date or time period.
- For category/count questions, specific entity names are CORRECT if they imply the same category or count requested by the gold answer.

Mark WRONG only when the generated answer contradicts the gold answer, misses an essential gold fact, gives a different entity/date/number, or says no information is available when the gold answer contains a specific fact.

# Input

Question: {{QUESTION}}
Gold answer: {{GOLD_ANSWER}}
Generated answer: {{GENERATED_ANSWER}}

# Output

Return ONLY one string, with no explanation, no punctuation, no markdown:
CORRECT
or
WRONG
